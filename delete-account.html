Delete Your WakaWay Account

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your WakaWay account (app package com.wakaway.app) and what happens to your data when you do.

Delete from inside the app

The fastest way, if you still have WakaWay installed:

  1. Open WakaWay and sign in.
  2. Go to You → the settings icon → Preferences.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out. There is no waiting period and no way to undo it.

Request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email wakawaytechnologies@gmail.com from the address you used to register, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account and sign-in credentials Deleted immediately and permanently
Your profile (email, display name) Deleted immediately and permanently
Saved places (Home, Work, favourites) Deleted immediately and permanently
Saved routes and trip history Deleted immediately and permanently
Recent searches and app preferences Stored only on your device; removed when you uninstall the app

What is kept, and why

Community reports you submitted — traffic, hazard, security and similar alerts — are unlinked from your account but stay on the map until they expire on their own, typically within 1 to 6 hours of being posted. The same applies to any confirm/dismiss votes you cast on other people's reports.

We do this because other travellers may be relying on a live alert at the moment you delete your account, and removing it could send someone into a road hazard or a security incident. Once unlinked, these records no longer contain your identity and cannot be traced back to you or to your email address.

If you specifically want your past reports removed as well, say so in your email and we will delete them along with the account.

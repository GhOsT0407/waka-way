Google Maps ends where Lagos actually begins.

Type Yaba to CMS into any mainstream maps app and you get a driving line and a bus icon that means nothing on the ground. It cannot tell you which danfo park to stand in, what the conductor will charge at 6pm, or that okada cannot legally carry you through Surulere.

WakaWay encodes that knowledge instead of pretending it is missing: 209 geocoded stops across the BRT spine, the Blue and Red Lines and the danfo hubs, priced with 2025 Lagos fares and re-priced for peak hours, night runs and the day's reported incidents.

How it routes

It thinks like someone who has done the trip before.

The engine chains walk, keke, okada, danfo, BRT and rail into one journey, then applies the rules Lagosians already apply without thinking.

First-mile pivot
If the walk to the nearest stop runs past 1.2 km, the route starts with a keke or okada instead of asking you to trek it.
Okada ban zones
Okada is never suggested inside the ten LGAs where Lagos State bans it — Ikeja, Lagos Island, Lagos Mainland, Eti-Osa, Apapa, Surulere, Mushin, Oshodi-Isolo, Kosofe and Somolu.
Peak and night pricing
Fares are re-priced for the rush windows — 7–9 am and 5–8 pm — and again for night runs, because the ₦300 morning drop is not a ₦300 drop at 9pm.
Incident avoidance
Routes bend around community-reported flooding, protests and breakdowns with a 300 m buffer around the report.
Difficulty, stated plainly
Easy is two transit legs or fewer, Moderate is three, Complex is four or more — so a newcomer can pay ₦200 more to avoid a triple transfer with luggage.
Pidgin per leg
Every leg carries the sentence that actually works at the park: “Enter danfo for Ojuelegba side, tell conductor ‘CMS!’”

Community reports

Riders report it. The app checks it.

Flooding at Ojuelegba, a protest on Ikorodu Road, a broken-down trailer at Mile 2 — riders report what they are looking at, and every report is run through AI verification before it is allowed to bend anyone's route.

Other riders confirm or dismiss it from the map, and a confirmed report widens the 300 m avoidance buffer for everybody travelling that corridor.

WakaWay Privacy Policy

Last updated: jun 21 2026

WakaWay ("we", "our", "the app") helps you find transit routes, fares, and community-reported road conditions in Lagos, Nigeria. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

DataWhy we collect itWhere it's stored
Email address & account ID To create and sign you into your account, and let you save routes Firebase Authentication (Google)
Device location (GPS) To show your position on the map, find nearby stops, and calculate routes and fares from where you are Processed on your device; only sent to our servers when you search a route or submit a report
Community reports you submit (type, description, location, time) To show live traffic, hazard, and security alerts to other users near that location Supabase (our database provider)
Saved places, recent searches, app preferences To make the app faster and remember your Home/Work shortcuts Stored locally on your device only — not sent to our servers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate routes, fares, and directions between two points in Lagos.
  • To show you and other nearby users live community alerts (traffic, hazards, security reports).
  • To let you save favourite routes and revisit your search history.
  • To keep your account secure and let you sign in across sessions.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not run ads and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

WakaWay relies on the following service providers to function. Each only receives the minimum data needed to do its job:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — manages sign-in and account credentials.
  • Supabase — stores community reports, saved routes, and account-linked data.
  • MapTiler — provides map tiles and place search/geocoding.
  • OpenRouteService — calculates walking/road-following directions.

These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually/technically restricted from using it for their own purposes.

4. Community Reports Are Visible to Other Users

When you submit a report (e.g. traffic, hazard, or security alert), its type, description, approximate location, and timestamp are visible to other WakaWay users on the map and alerts feed. Reports do not display your name, email, or account identity to other users.

5. Data Retention

  • Community reports automatically expire and are removed after a set time depending on report type (typically 1–6 hours), since road conditions change quickly.
  • Account data is kept until you delete your account or request removal (see Section 7).
  • Locally stored data (recent searches, preferences) stays on your device and is cleared if you uninstall the app.

6. Children's Privacy

WakaWay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent to location access at any time via your device settings — note that route search and nearby-stop features will not work without it.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
